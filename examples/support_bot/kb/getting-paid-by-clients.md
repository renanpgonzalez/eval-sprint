# Getting paid by clients

## Connect a payment account
To let clients pay online, connect a payment account at Settings > Payments. Clients can pay by card, by ACH (US only) or by SEPA debit (EU only).

## Payouts
Payouts reach your bank account in 2 business days.

## Fees
Card payments cost 2.9% + $0.30 per transaction. ACH payments cost 0.8%, capped at $5. These fees are charged by our payment processor, and Ledgerly adds no fee on top.

## Marking an invoice as paid
If a client pays you outside Ledgerly, for example by bank transfer, cash or check, open the invoice and choose Mark as paid.
