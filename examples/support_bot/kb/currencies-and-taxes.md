# Currencies and taxes

## Currencies
You can invoice each client in any of 35 supported currencies. Ledgerly does not convert between currencies: each invoice stays in the currency you chose.

## Tax rates
You can add up to 2 tax rates per line item, for example VAT plus a local tax. Tax rates are managed under Settings > Invoicing > Taxes.

## VAT ID
Add your VAT ID under Settings > Invoicing > Business details and it appears on every invoice. You can also store a client's VAT ID on their client page.

## Tax filing
Ledgerly does not file or remit taxes for you. It only calculates the tax shown on each invoice, so check your filing obligations with an accountant.
