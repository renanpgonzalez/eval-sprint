# Exporting and deleting your data

## Export to CSV
Go to Settings > Data > Export CSV to download your clients, invoices and payments as CSV files.

## Bulk PDF download
Select invoices in the Invoices list and choose Download PDFs to get one ZIP file with up to 500 invoices.

## Delete your account
Go to Settings > Account > Delete account. Deleting your account is permanent, and we purge your data within 30 days. Export your data first, because it can't be recovered afterwards.
