# Recurring invoices

## Availability
Recurring invoices are available on the Pro and Team plans.

## Setting up a schedule
Open Invoices > New invoice, turn on Repeat, and choose weekly, monthly, quarterly or yearly. Turn on Auto-send to email each new invoice to your client automatically on its issue date.

## Pausing and resuming
You can pause a recurring schedule at any time and resume it later. A paused schedule does not create invoices.

## Editing a schedule
Changes to a recurring invoice apply to future invoices only. Invoices that were already created or sent stay as they are.
