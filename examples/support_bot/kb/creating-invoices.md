# Creating invoices

## Create an invoice
1. Go to Invoices > New invoice.
2. Choose a client, or add a new one.
3. Add line items, a due date and any tax rates.
4. Select Send, or Save as draft to finish later.

## Invoice numbering
Ledgerly numbers your invoices automatically. To change the prefix, for example INV- or 2026-, go to Settings > Invoicing > Numbering.

## Editing and deleting invoices
You can edit or delete a draft at any time. Once an invoice has been sent, it can't be deleted. Void it instead: open the invoice and choose Void. A voided invoice stays in your records with its number. To fix a mistake on a sent invoice, void it and create a corrected one.
