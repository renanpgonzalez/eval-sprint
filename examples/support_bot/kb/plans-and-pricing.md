# Plans and pricing

## Overview
Ledgerly has three plans: Starter, Pro and Team. You can change plans at any time.

## Starter
Starter is free ($0). It includes up to 5 clients and 10 invoices per month.

## Pro
Pro costs $15 per month, or $150 per year if you pay annually, which saves you $30 a year. Pro includes unlimited clients and invoices, recurring invoices and custom branding.

## Team
Team costs $39 per month and includes 3 seats. Each extra seat costs $9 per month. Team adds roles and permissions on top of everything in Pro.

## Changing your plan
Go to Settings > Billing > Change plan and choose the plan you want. Upgrades take effect immediately, and you pay a prorated amount for the rest of the billing cycle. Downgrades take effect at the end of your current billing cycle, so you keep your current features until then.
