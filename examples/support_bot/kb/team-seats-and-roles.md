# Team seats and roles

## Seats
The Team plan includes 3 seats, and extra seats cost $9 per month each. Everyone you invite uses one seat, whatever their role.

## Roles
Ledgerly has four roles: Owner, Admin, Accountant and Member. Owner has full control, including billing. Admin manages everything except billing and account deletion. Accountant has read-only access plus export. Member can create and edit invoices and clients.

## Inviting people
Go to Settings > Team, choose Invite and pick a role.

## Removing people
Removing someone ends their access immediately. The seat is removed from your bill from the next billing cycle.
