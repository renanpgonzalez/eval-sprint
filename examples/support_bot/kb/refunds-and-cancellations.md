# Refunds and cancellations

## Annual plans
Annual plans are refundable in full within 14 days of purchase or renewal. After 14 days, annual plans are not refunded.

## Monthly plans
Monthly plans are not refundable. You can cancel at any time and keep access until the end of the period you already paid for.

## What is not refunded
We don't refund partial months or unused seats.

## Cancelling your subscription
To cancel, move to the Starter plan at Settings > Billing > Change plan. Your paid features stay active until the end of the current period.

## How to request a refund
Send the support form with your invoice number, which you can find under Settings > Billing > History. Approved refunds reach your original payment method in 5-10 business days.
