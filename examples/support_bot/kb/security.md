# Security

## Two-factor authentication
Turn on two-factor authentication (2FA) under Settings > Account > Security. Ledgerly supports authenticator apps only. SMS codes are not supported.

## Single sign-on
SAML single sign-on (SSO) is available on the Team plan only.

## Data protection
Your data is encrypted with AES-256 at rest and with TLS in transit.

## Password reset
Choose Forgot password on the sign-in page. The reset link is valid for 1 hour. After that, request a new one.
