# Contacting support

## Support hours
Our support team is available Monday to Friday, 9:00-18:00 CET.

## Chat and email
Use the chat in the app or email support@ledgerly.example.com. You can also send the support form under Help > Contact support.

## Team plan priority
Team plan customers get a priority response within 4 business hours.

## Talking to a person
In this chat, type "agent" to be handed to a human teammate during support hours.
