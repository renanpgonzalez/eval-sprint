# Billing and payment methods

## Billing contact
Ledgerly sends subscription receipts and payment notices to your billing email. To change it, go to Settings > Billing > Billing contact.

## Subscription invoices
Your Ledgerly subscription invoices and receipts are under Settings > Billing > History. You can download each one as a PDF.

## Payment methods
We accept Visa, Mastercard and American Express cards. Annual plans can also be paid by bank transfer. To update your card, go to Settings > Billing > Payment method.

## Failed payments
If a subscription payment fails, we retry it 3 times over 7 days and email your billing contact each time. If every retry fails, your account becomes read-only: you can still view and export your data, but you can't create or send invoices. Update your card and full access returns as soon as a retry succeeds.
