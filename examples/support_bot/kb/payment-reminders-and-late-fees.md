# Payment reminders and late fees

## Automatic reminders
Turn on reminders at Settings > Reminders. You can schedule up to 3 reminders per invoice, before or after the due date.

## Late fees
You can add an optional late fee, either a percentage of the invoice or a fixed amount. A late fee is applied once, when the invoice becomes overdue, and is not repeated.

## Stopping reminders
Reminders stop automatically when an invoice is paid or marked as paid. You can also pause reminders for a single invoice from its detail page.
